## Overview
The "Meza Ora de Bora" is a local wingfoil and windsurf event taking place in Sistiana (TS), organized by Diporto Nautico di Sistiana and Reptile Sports. This 3rd edition will take place some day (a Saturday or a Sunday) between 17 October and 15 November 2026, depending on the wind condition.

## Conditions
The competition date will be communicated with 2-3 days of advance (🟡 Yellow Alert) and will be confirmed the day before (🟢 Green Alert) if at least 15kts are forcasted and a minimum of 20 pre-subscriptions is reached. I'll try to keep this page updated accordingly, but for more information follow the official Instagram page: https://www.instagram.com/mezaoradebora/
