## Overview

Three years into a relationship I decide to gift a chart to my girlfriend. After spending countless hours trying to reconstruct our rough locations on a day-to-day basis for the last three years (I know, sounds quite psycho...) I represent the data as two lines moving closer or further apart as time goes by (representing geographical distance), color-coding each moment based on categories like "trip"/"home" etc.

Printed on a 140cmx56cm canvas.
