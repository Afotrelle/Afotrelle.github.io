## Overview

This project explored how carefully crafted perturbations can destabilize models trained on video data. The goal was not only to show the model can be fooled, but to understand the structure of the failure and communicate it clearly.

## What I explored

- robustness evaluation under distribution shifts
- perturbation sensitivity in temporal representations
- model diagnostics through interpretable visual summaries
- practical reporting for research collaborators

## Key takeaway

The most important lesson was that robustness is as much about measurement and communication as it is about model architecture. A model that appears strong in aggregate can still fail in specific, systematic ways.
